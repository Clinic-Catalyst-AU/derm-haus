The consultation

One hour. Your skin, your history, and a plan built for you.

Every new client starts here, for skin and for medical aesthetics. The $100 deposit is credited towards retail or treatment on the day. You leave with a written treatment plan, and we follow it up with you, not just email it and hope.

01

We look

Skin analysis, history, what you have tried, what has and has not worked, and what is driving the concern underneath.

02

We plan

A written treatment plan: the pathway, the order, homecare and what to expect. Sent to you the same day.

03

We follow up

A text the next day, education on your pathway, and a call from your clinic coordinator if you have questions. No chasing, no pressure.

04

We keep going

Your next treatment is booked before you leave. Skin and medical aesthetics work together, so your plan can move between them as your skin changes.

Our dermal therapists

A special interest in pigmentation, texture and skin health. Your skin consultation starts with them.

Do I need to know what treatment I want?

No. Tell us what you are noticing. The consultation works out the pathway, and we explain every option before anything is booked.

What happens to my $100 deposit?

It is credited towards retail or treatment on the day of your consultation. If you decide not to go ahead, it covers the consultation itself.

Can I see results before I book?

Skin results are shared with consent on this site. Results for prescription-only treatments are shared privately after your consultation, in line with Australian advertising rules.

Which clinic should I choose?

Whichever is closer. Annerley on the south side, Bald Hills on the north side, the same team and the same plan.
