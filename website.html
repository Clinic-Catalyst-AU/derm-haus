Do I need to know what treatment I want?

No. Tell us what you are noticing. The consultation works out the pathway, and every option is explained before anything is booked.

What happens to my $100 deposit?

It is credited towards retail or treatment on the day of your consultation.

Can I see results before I book?

Skin treatment journeys are in the results gallery. Results for prescription-only treatments are shared privately after your consultation, in line with Australian advertising rules.

Which clinic should I choose?

Whichever is closer. Annerley on the south side, Bald Hills on the north side.
